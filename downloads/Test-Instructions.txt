Mindful Heart 1.84, build 66
Family beta test. Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 13.5 or later.
Intel and older supported macOS runtime testing is still pending.

INSTALL
1. Download from https://theoudatadynamics.github.io/mindful-heart/beta.html.
2. Double-click the ZIP, then move Mindful Heart.app into Applications.
3. Open the app and finish the welcome steps.

This is for a fresh installation. If this Mac has an older unsandboxed Mindful Menu or Mindful Heart 1.52
with saved data, stop before replacing it. Migration testing is unfinished.
Earlier sandboxed betas should use Check for Updates in the app.

IF APPLE CANNOT VERIFY MINDFUL HEART
You may see "Mindful Heart Not Opened" and a message that Apple could not verify
the app is free of malware. This beta has not been notarized by Apple.

If you trust this download and choose to open it:
1. Click Done in the warning.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to the message about Mindful Heart and click Open Anyway.
4. Authenticate with your Mac password or Touch ID if asked.
5. Click Open in the confirmation dialog.

macOS saves an exception for this app, so you can normally open it afterwards
by double-clicking it. Apple's documented steps:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

Do not disable Gatekeeper or run Terminal commands to remove security protections.
If the warning says the app contains malware, will damage your computer, or is
damaged, stop and report the exact message instead. If Open Anyway is unavailable
on a managed Mac, contact its administrator.

TRY THESE
- Change the theme accent and check the heart in the sidebar tagline.
- Open the Quote tab with short and long quotes and check the bottom controls.
- Open another app full screen and check that the Mindful Heart panel opens in that Space.
- Complete a daily habit in Wellness and hide and restore a reminder.
- Switch between Quote, Reminders, Wellness and Clipboard in both directions.
  The panel should keep its size. Scroll inside Reminders and Wellness.
- Preview a quote overlay and try Copy or Save. Hover to keep it open.
- In the quote panel, revisit a previous quote, then hide and restore a quote.
  In Wellness, check upcoming nudges and undo a snooze. Try a short pause.
- Connect Apple Reminders if you wish, then create a reminder with a due time.
- Copy a few harmless items and check that the newest unpinned item leads Recent.
- In the menu-bar panel, open Reminders. Check the next nudge or pause reason,
  snooze a due Mindful Heart reminder for 10 minutes, and try Open Wellness.
- Try a wide display and then return to the laptop display. The quote should
  use the space available. Read the full quote with Option-Command-M while
  Mindful Heart is active. Check the next nudge or pause reason in Wellness.
- Can you open Quotes, search, save a favourite, quit and reopen with it intact?
- Does a wellness reminder appear briefly and leave the normal quote unchanged?
- Create a harmless test snippet. Edit, rename, save, pin and categorise it.
- Open the clipboard picker with Control-Option-V, copy a test item, then paste
  into TextEdit with Command-V.
- Try light/dark mode, countdown visibility, and sleep/wake.
- On an earlier sandboxed beta, use Check for Updates to get 1.84. Confirm your saved items remain.
- On 1.84 build 66, Check for Updates should report that you are up to date.

PRIVACY
Clipboard capture starts off. Enable it only if you want future copies stored
locally. Use sample text for testing. This ZIP contains no developer clipboard
library or preferences. The beta has no automatic analytics receiver.

OPTIONAL USAGE TOTALS
Update consent does not enable usage collection. If you choose, open Settings >
Privacy & beta and enable Collect optional usage totals on this Mac. After using
the app, open See exactly what is recorded, review the counts, then choose Export
usage totals. Share the JSON file with your beta contact only if you want to.
Do not send a clipboard-library backup. Nothing is sent automatically.

WHAT IS NEW IN 1.65
- Use Next quote several times. Selected categories should participate, and each
  eligible quote should be used once before repeating. A two-quote pool alternates.
- Settings > Quote rotation shows the actual available count. Use broader quote
  mix expands a small selection explicitly; existing choices are never expanded
  silently. ESV/NASB full text remains unavailable pending permission.
- Note the current quote/countdown, quit normally, then reopen before it is due.
  The same quote and remaining deadline should be kept.
- With Speak quotes enabled, lock/unlock or sleep/wake. Return should stay quiet.
  The next ordinary interval may speak; Test voice/explicit Speak still works.
- While a quote is speaking, use Pause rotation. Speech should stop immediately.
  Resume should not replay the interrupted quote. Call-aware protection remains.
- Previous language-aware speech, independent wellness deadline and Settings
  reopening repairs remain in this release. iPhone development builds are separate.

FEATURES RETAINED FROM 1.57
Apple Reminders permission is repaired. Connect it in Settings > Reminders and
approve macOS access yourself. If you previously denied access, enable it in
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Reminders. Return to the app to recheck.
If Today shows no matches, try All Upcoming or another list. Preview nudge lets
you check delivery without waiting for its scheduled interval.

Wellness keeps breaks, water, breathing and posture available. Restore default
nudges adds missing presets without changing your existing reminders. Add a
personal reminder for your own words, at daily times, an interval or once after
a delay. These local reminders need the app running and follow Pause and active
hours. They do not need Apple Reminders access.

Clipboard setup asks you to choose automatic history or saved snippets only.
Automatic capture stays off until you enable it. Existing choices are preserved.
If macOS blocks clipboard access, review Privacy & Security and copy a new item.
Do not use confidential information to test history.

Quote overlays allow reading time based on text length, up to two minutes. Your selected duration is the minimum. Hover HeartSpace to pause dismissal or use its close button when finished. Settings > Appearance > Allow time to read can turn this off. Wellness nudges keep their separate timing.

Memorise a quote or verse
- Open Memorise in the menu-bar popover or sidebar of the Mac app.
- Create a collection, then use Add to collection for the current quote. Use the Collections menu while practising a quote or verse to file it.
- Choose Easy, Medium or Hard. Check your score after Missing words or Phrase order, or rate your own Recall round. The review deck keeps each card’s latest score.

Type test and reminder types
- In Memorise, open a quote or verse and choose Type test. Enter each word, correct a mismatch, or choose Study again to reread the full text.
- In Reminders, choose a type such as Call or Meeting when adding an item. Apple Reminders handles its due-time alert when notifications are enabled.

Menu bar reminder cue
- Connect Apple Reminders in the Reminders tab, then add a timed reminder.
- With the defaults, the menu bar shows a bell and countdown from five minutes before the due time. Choose another lead time or Off in Settings → Reminders.

Resume an unfinished reminder
- Open Reminders in the menu-bar popover and start a draft.
- Close and reopen the popover. The Reminders tab and draft return. The draft stays in memory until you save it, cancel it, or quit the app.

At the reminder’s due time
- HeartSpace opens and the menu-bar cue shows Now. You can turn off the overlay in Settings → Reminders.
- Turn on Ring for 10 seconds when due to hear a short chime repeated over ten seconds. Click the menu-bar item or dismiss the overlay to silence it.

Try a reminder alert
- In Settings → Reminders, click Test Alert to see the sample overlay and hear the chime. Dismiss the overlay to silence it.
- For a single timed reminder, click Snooze 5 min to schedule a local follow-up or Mark as Done to complete it in Apple Reminders.

Clipboard popover
- Open Clipboard with Command-5. Type in the already-focused search field.
- Select an item with Up or Down. Press Space to preview it or Return to copy and go back to your app. Press Command-V there to paste.
- Hover over an item to preview, save, pin, or move it to Recently Deleted. Web links also have an Open link action.
